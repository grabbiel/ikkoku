# Ikkoku asset pipeline (Track A)

Everything under `Assets/` is generated by the scripts in this directory from
CC0 MakeHuman sources. Nothing is hand-edited. The contract the outputs satisfy
is `docs/ASSET_SPEC.md`.

## Rebuild

```
Tools/assets/run_all.sh                 # everything (~10 min on an M-series Mac)
Tools/assets/run_all.sh body            # a single stage: body | textures | hair | clothes | items | catalog | verify
STAGES="hair verify" Tools/assets/run_all.sh
```

Requirements: Blender 5.1 at `/Applications/Blender.app` (override with
`BLENDER=`), and a python3 with numpy + Pillow (`PY=`, default
`/opt/homebrew/bin/python3`; the pyenv shim on this machine resolves to a
python without numpy inside some directories). Blender runs with
`--factory-startup` so no user add-ons are loaded. Every script is
deterministic (fixed random seeds) and re-runnable; logs go to `out/*.log`.

Individual scripts (all accept `--` arguments when run through Blender):

| script | runs in | produces |
|---|---|---|
| `build_body.py --sex f\|m` | Blender | `Assets/Characters/body_<sex>.glb`, `.regions.bin`, `out/body_<sex>.npz` (data for the other stages), `out/body_<sex>_summary.json` |
| `textures.py` | python3 | `Assets/Textures/*` (skin, detail, face overlays, eyes, lashes, brows, hair strand, patterns) + `out/tex/*` (converted CC0 hair/cloth maps that get embedded) |
| `build_hair.py --sex f --only ...` | Blender | `Assets/Hair/hair_<style>.glb` |
| `build_clothes.py --sex f\|m` | Blender | `Assets/Clothes/<slot>_<name>.glb` + `<file>_cm.png` (ColorMask) + `<file>_bm.png` (body mask) |
| `build_items.py` | Blender | `Assets/Accessories/acc_*.glb`, `Assets/Items/item_*.glb` |
| `catalog.py` | python3 | `Assets/catalog.json` |
| `verify.py body f` / `hair` / `clothes` / `accessories` / `items`; `verify.py sheet` (python3) | Blender / python3 | `out/verify/*.png` |

Library modules: `common.py` (OBJ/target/mhclo loaders, proxy fitting, mesh
utilities, GLB parser, PNG writer, UV rasteriser, Blender helpers),
`landmarks.py` (mesh + joint-helper derived feature points), `deform.py`
(mask/op library, anime restyle, the morph-target table), `rig.py` (skeleton
table, bone-heat + numpy fallback weights, regions).

## Sources

`source/` is git-ignored. It holds the eight CC0 GLBs from
`~/Downloads/eight-human-models/models` (kept for reference — their bodies
have the skin under the clothing deleted, so they are not used as the body
source) and `source/makehuman/`, copied from the same project's
`source-assets/makehuman` folder: the hm08 `base.obj`, the macro targets, and
the MakeHuman system asset pack (eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, teeth, tongue,
hair, clothes) plus the Toigo camisole dress. All CC0 1.0 (see
`source/makehuman/LICENSE.md`, `source/ASSET_LICENSES.md`). The paid
"Customisable Basemesh" add-on is never loaded (`--factory-startup`) and none
of its meshes are used.

## How the body is built

1. `base.obj` (13,380 body vertices, quads, MakeHuman UV layout) + the
   `caucasian-<sex>-young` macro target. Helper geometry (joint cubes, eye /
   teeth / tongue / hair / tights / skirt helpers) is kept and deformed along
   with the body so every proxy can be refit afterwards.
2. Landmarks (`landmarks.py`): joint positions are the centroids of the 125
   MakeHuman `joint-*` helper cubes; eye centres/radii are sphere fits of the
   eye helper shells; the lip contact line is found from vertex pairs with
   opposite vertical normals; nose tip / subnasale / nasion / chin from the
   sagittal profile; ears / jaw corners / cheeks / brows / bust / navel / hips
   from band extrema.
3. Anime restyle (`deform.restyle_anime`), applied to all 19,158 vertices:
   legs +6 % (re-grounded), limbs −8 % radial, neck ×0.85, head ×1.15 about the
   neck base, eyes ×1.5 scaled about the eyeball's **front pole** (so the lid
   surface keeps its depth and the spherical eyeball grows into the skull),
   nose ×0.65 about its midpoint (+ nostril smoothing), mouth ×0.85, cheek
   fill, socket-ring smoothing, lid margins rotated about the eye centre
   (upper +18°, lower −8°) for a tall anime opening, 2 Taubin-smoothing
   iterations on the face.
4. Proxies (eyes, eyelashes, eyebrows, teeth, tongue) are refit through their
   `.mhclo` vertex references against the restyled mesh. The eyeball
   (inner 276-vertex sphere of the high-poly eye) is rescaled so the lid /
   eyeball gap ratio equals the source's, split into `eye_L`/`eye_R`, and given
   a fresh planar UV projection along the gaze axis (iris centred).
5. Rig (`rig.py`): 58 bones named per the spec; weights from Blender bone heat
   (`ARMATURE_AUTO`), bust bones blended in radially, limited to 4 influences
   and normalised; numpy inverse-distance fallback if heat leaves vertices
   uncovered (it did not).
6. Morph targets: 74 targets from `deform.morph_table` evaluated on the body
   (with normals) and — for the relevant subset — on the eyeballs, eyelashes
   and eyebrows, so lashes follow blinks. Lids (blink, wide, squint, smile,
   lid sliders) are rotations about the eye centre so margins stay on the
   eyeball.
7. `_REGION` custom attribute (INT on the mesh; the glTF exporter writes it as
   a FLOAT scalar accessor) + `body_<sex>.regions.bin` sidecar (uint8 per
   exported vertex, order verified by position matching).
8. Per-vertex ambient occlusion (BVH ray casts) and concavity are saved for the
   texture stage.

## Textures

Near-white where tinted at runtime: skin base (AO + concavity shading, warm
cheeks / knees / elbows / fingertips, navel), detail map (R specular, G shading
detail, B drawn lines: collarbones, under-bust, ankles), face overlays (alpha
masks in body UV space: blush, eyeshadow, lip), three iris variants (alpha
outside the iris, dark pupil, rim, lower highlight crescent, streaks), three
highlight shapes, sclera with upper shadow, eyelash strips (bold band + flicks /
fine) and eyebrow strips derived from the CC0 MakeHuman alpha silhouettes,
hair strand map (u across, v root→tip, tip alpha), five tiling patterns.

Face: the eyelid loops get 3 extra Taubin iterations and the opening is tapered
into an almond (`deform.restyle_anime` step 7d); the eyeball is moved up to 3 mm
toward the skin and re-fit to the lids (`build_body.EYE_FORWARD`, shrink capped
at 8 %). The eyelash / eyebrow textures are painted in the strips' own UV frame
(root -> tip derived from the refit proxy meshes): thin upper liner thickening
to the outer corner with 6-8 lashes there, faint lower line; clean tapered brow.

## Hair, clothes, accessories, items

See the docstrings at the top of `build_hair.py`, `build_clothes.py`,
`build_items.py`. Every hair / cloth GLB contains a copy of the body skeleton
(same bone names, rest pose) plus `hair_back_01..n` chains for long styles.

Clothes specifics:
* layer offsets (`build_clothes.OFFSETS`): bra/underwear 2 mm, pantyhose/gloves
  1.5 mm, socks 2.5 mm (worn over pantyhose, so +1 mm), tops/bottoms/skirt 7 mm,
  blazer/dress 10 mm, shoes 5 mm. Refit MakeHuman garments are pushed to at
  least that distance from the skin (`enforce_offset`).
* skin weights come from the nearest body vertex *of the slot's regions only*
  (`WEIGHT_REGIONS`; bottoms -> torso_lower/thighs/calves, tops -> torso/arms,
  gloves -> hands/forearms, socks/shoes -> calves/feet).
* `<file>_bm.png` body mask (1024², body UV space, white = hidden): a body vertex
  is covered when the ray along its normal and a 50° cone of 8 rays around it all
  hit a same-facing garment face within the slot's reach (or the garment is
  within 2 mm), it is not within 1.5 cm of an opening edge, and it lies inside
  the garment's height range (bottoms: 3 cm above the hem). `hideBody` = regions
  with >= 90 % of their vertices covered. `verify.py clothes` deletes the masked
  body faces before rendering so poke-through would show.
* `<file>_cm.png` ColorMask (1024²): background = zone 1 (red), islands dilated
  4 px; procedural parts (sailor collar/ribbon, blazer buttons) are packed into a
  free block of the atlas so they never overlap the garment's islands (the block
  is painted white in the per-garment luminance texture).
* the pleated skirt is lofted from the hips: waist ring = hip circumference +
  1 cm at z = 1.00, every ring kept >= 7 mm + pleat depth outside the torso/legs
  (arms are ignored), hem 0.29 x 0.27 m at z = 0.62, 24 knife pleats with sharp
  fold edges.

Accessories are placed on the body_f skin (ribbon on the side-top of the head with
its bow tangent to the skin, beret rim where the head is 0.112 m wide, bag beside
the hip with the strap over the shoulder); glasses lenses use `ik_glass`
(alphaMode BLEND, alpha 0.3).

## Verification

`out/verify/`: `body_<sex>_{front,side,back}.png`, `body_<sex>_rig_{front,side,hand}.png`
(bones drawn through an x-ray body), `body_<sex>_face_*.png`,
`body_<sex>_eyes_closeup*.png` (Eevee, textured), `morphs_<sex>_<group>.png`
contact sheets (every target at +1), `hair_<style>{,_34,_back}.png`,
`<garment>{,_back}.png` (body mask applied), `acc_<name>{,_front}.png`
(placed at the declared bone + offset), `items_lineup.png`, room renders.
`verify.py clothes|hair|accessories <glob>` renders a subset.
